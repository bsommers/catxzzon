# How to play CATXZZON

You are Major Marmalade, flying as a cat through four stages toward Big Boss Jesse. The view is isometric: forward is up-right on screen, and height is the extra dimension that kills you if you ignore it.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Steer left/right | Left/Right or A/D | lateral movement across the field |
| Climb / dive | Up/Down or W/S | altitude |
| Fire | Space or Z | hold for autofire if enabled in Options |
| Yarn bomb | X or Shift | clears small enemies on screen, damages the boss; you start with 2 |
| Pause | P or Esc | |

Gamepad (standard mapping) and on-screen touch controls are supported; the game switches to whichever device you used last.

## Reading the screen

- **Shadow:** the dark blob under the cat shows your ground position. Line it up with targets and gaps.
- **ALT meter (left):** your altitude. Walls and force fields have a height; the meter tells you whether you clear them.
- **FUEL bar (bottom):** drains over time. Shoot tuna tins to refill it. Run dry and you are in trouble.
- **Lives and bombs** are shown bottom-left.

## Hazards and targets

- **Walls and battlements** of cheese: fly over, or through gaps.
- **Force fields** (spray cheese between posts): cross at the wrong height and you zap. Shoot the posts or go over/under.
- **Rat turrets, jets and patrols:** shoot them; destroy several quickly for combo callouts.
- **Space potatoes** tumble through the Spud Belt. Dodge the big ones.
- **Catnip** triggers rainbow overdrive (a short power-up). Yarn pickups give +1 bomb.

## Stages

| # | Stage | Kind |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cheddar Citadel | fortress |
| 2 | Spud Belt | space |
| 3 | Gorgonzola Keep | fortress |
| 4 | Jesse's Lair | boss fight |

After the lair the rounds repeat with a new seed and more pressure.

## Tips

1. Stay low near walls so shots hit; climb only when a field or wall forces you.
2. Watch the shadow, not the cat.
3. Save bombs for the boss and for crowds.
4. Use `?bot=1` in the URL to watch the autopilot play, and `?stage=N` (0 to 3) to jump to a stage.
